Muskogee (Creek) Publications

Fieldwork, Reference Grammars, Dictionaries, and Texts

On Muskogee, I have worked closely with native speakers in Oklahoma and Florida, in particular the late Margaret McKane Mauldin and her sister Juanita McGirt, with Chumona Deere and staff at the Muscogee (Creek) Nation, and with the Seminole Nation’s Pumvhakv Immersion School.

Dedicated Language Portal: Visit the Muskogee Language Portal (muskogee.pages.wm.edu) for online dictionaries, audio recordings, and learning materials.

Link Color Scheme: In accordance with access status, Green buttons are strictly reserved for free, open-access sources (such as downloadable PDFs, open-access journal articles, and open online archives). Gray buttons are reserved for paid or subscription-access digital editions (such as JSTOR, University of Oklahoma Press, University of Nebraska Press, UChicago Press, and John Benjamins).
