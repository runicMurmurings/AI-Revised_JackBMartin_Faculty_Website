Cross-Linguistic & Multi-Language Publications

Comparative Muskogean, Southeastern Areal Linguistics, Siouan, Multilingualism, and Typology

This section compiles works that investigate more than one language, including comparative reconstructions of Proto-Muskogean morphology and phonology, surveys of indigenous languages of the American Southeast, multilingual contact studies, comparative Siouan phonology (Crow and Hidatsa), and general cross-linguistic syntactic typology.
