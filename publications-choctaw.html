Choctaw Publications & Lexicography

Morphosyntax, Dictionaries, and Community Documentation

I have worked with the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma on a modern dictionary of Choctaw. Some students and I also edited a new digital edition of Cyrus Byington’s Choctaw dictionary accessible via the William & Mary Choctaw portal. In 2020 I began working with the Mississippi Band of Choctaw Indians on oral history and lexicography.

Dedicated Language Portal: Visit the Choctaw Language Portal (choctaw.pages.wm.edu) for dictionary access and linguistic resources.

Link Color Scheme: Green buttons are reserved for free open-access materials (such as full dictionary PDFs and open language portals). Citations without buttons indicate works where no direct digital copy is currently available.
