Language Portals & Community Projects

Digital Archives, Searchable Lexicons, and Collaborative Revitalization Sites

Much of my work is collaborative, conducted in partnership with Native nations, cultural departments, elders, and educators. Below are the primary web portals and digital documentation projects developed to support language maintenance, pedagogy, and research.

Muskogee (Creek) Language Portal

A comprehensive web resource featuring an online searchable dictionary, grammatical guides, orthography descriptions, sound files, and archival materials.

Explore muskogee.pages.wm.edu →

Koasati Language Portal

Created in collaboration with the Coushatta Tribe of Louisiana and Coushatta Heritage Department, supporting community documentation and revitalization.

Explore koasati.pages.wm.edu →

Choctaw Language Portal

Hosts historical lexicography, including Cyrus Byington’s dictionary project, and language data developed with Choctaw language teachers.

Explore choctaw.pages.wm.edu →

Totkv Mocvse (New Fire) Creek Folktales

Earnest Gouge's Creek folktales with original Creek text, English translations, and cultural commentary, preserved in collaboration with Margaret McKane Mauldin and Juanita McGirt.

Explore Digital Edition (Project MUSE) →

Byington's Choctaw Dictionary Online

A digital modern re-edition of Cyrus Byington’s classic Dictionary of the Choctaw Language, prepared with students at William & Mary.

Access via Choctaw Portal →

New Choctaw Dictionary (PDF)

Modern dictionary compiled with the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma School of Choctaw Language for classroom and community use.

Download Free Dictionary PDF →

Community Collaborators & Partner Institutions

  • Muscogee (Creek) Nation – Collaborative dictionary work, teacher workshops, and pedagogical materials.
  • Seminole Nation Pumvhakv Immersion School – Early childhood immersion language documentation and training.
  • Ah-Tah-Thi-Ki Museum (Seminole Tribe of Florida) – A Dictionary of Miccosukee (Elaponke) with Carol Cypress and Virginia Tommie.
  • Ahfachkee School – Language classes and immersion teaching on the Big Cypress Reservation.
  • Coushatta Heritage Department – The Koasati Language Project with Linda Langley and Bertney Langley.
  • Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma – School of Choctaw Language and lexicographical tools.
  • Mississippi Band of Choctaw Indians – Ongoing oral history and lexicographical documentation since 2020.
