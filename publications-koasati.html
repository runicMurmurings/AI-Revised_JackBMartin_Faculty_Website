Koasati Publications

Fieldwork, Phonetics, Revitalization, and Community Collaboration in Louisiana

On Koasati, I’ve collaborated with Linda Langley, Bertney Langley, and the Coushatta Heritage Department in Elton, Louisiana. Research topics have centered on community-based revitalization, acoustic and prosodic phonetics, and grammatical negation.

Dedicated Language Portal: Explore the Koasati Language Portal (koasati.pages.wm.edu) for grammar, recordings, and archival materials.
