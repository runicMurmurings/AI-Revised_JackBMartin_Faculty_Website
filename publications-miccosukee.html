Miccosukee (Mikasuki / Hitchiti) Publications

Lexicography and Phonology of the Florida Seminole and Miccosukee Communities

On Miccosukee, I’ve worked with Carol Cypress and Virginia Tommie at the Seminole Tribe’s Ah-Tah-Thi-Ki Museum, and with Jeannette Cypress and Mary Jene Koenes at Ahfachkee School on the Big Cypress Reservation in Florida.
